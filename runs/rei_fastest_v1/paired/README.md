# Prescribed discrete paired histories

This directory contains 15 independently checked homogeneous FLRW, Bianchi I and weak-shear histories for the fixed S0 model on normal time [0, 1e13] seconds. Case-A HG recombination/collisional ionization, two DR terms and the primary 13.7 eV line source are selected; HH, RCT and CR are OFF. The campaign validates the declared discrete numerical map. Physical fits, continuum error bounds, observational reionization and volume filling QV remain unadmitted.

`campaign_summary.json` records the actual full-history proof results. `paired_history.csv` contains final values; `curves/*.csv.gz` contain accepted endpoint curves; `difference_error_ledger.json` separates measured time, spectral and angular differences and the resolution dependence of the Bianchi minus FLRW residual. These differences are not rigorous continuum or physical uncertainty bounds. `paired_history.png` and `.pdf` show the paired baseline and weak-shear control; run `python3 plot_paired_history.py` to regenerate the figure from published curves.

`source_manifest.json`, `source_identities/` and `receipts/` bind each result to its actual source, full raw history hash, header, final checkpoint and independent proof. Complete JSONL histories remain at the listed local paths; they are not duplicated in Git. Original failed T2 candidates and their partial proofs remain preserved. The successful T2 variant uses a cancellation-safe conservative thermal increment with checkpointed floating-point compensation and unchanged model, root map and 1e-12 conservation gate. Canonical packaging has separate qualification; it does not replace the sources that actually produced the campaign.

`consumer_inputs/*-electron_density.csv.gz` exports proper electron densities in cm^-3 at actual normal-time endpoints, using nH*xHII+nHe*(xHeII+2*xHeIII). Bounds are conditional on the binary stage density and certified gas boxes. D=1 applies to this zero-tilt normal observer. Observer tails, Thomson cross-section authority and receiver visibility integration require separate receiver inputs/execution. A passive lower spectral guard is discretization export, not a physical source-tail measurement.
