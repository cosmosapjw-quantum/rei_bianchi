The most directly reusable idea for all-isotope-ion LRS Bianchi I is **structure-aware derivative supply and measured callback accounting**. The facade accepts analytic dense or banded Jacobian, JVP, and grouped finite differences; banded finite differences require `min(lower+upper+1,n)+1` RHS calls per matrix versus `n+1` dense, if the verified coupling pattern is truly banded. The banded fast path factors at `O(n bandwidth²)` according to its documented route; mass or strict sequential dense path can assemble the band into dense `O(n³)` factors. JVP-only cannot currently combine with facade dense output, mass, or vector absolute tolerance. A species network with global radiation, charge or expansion couplings might be sparse yet *not banded* under naive ordering. Derive the actual dependency graph before choosing a band or JVP path; do not zero physical couplings to force bandedness.
