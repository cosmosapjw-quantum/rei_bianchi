Let a nonnegative inclusive outgoing electron measure μ on (ε,n), ε≥0, n∈S², have ∫dμ=2 and finite K=∫ε dμ. Define S=∫ε nnᵀdμ and T=S−(K/3)I. Then S is real symmetric positive semidefinite with tr S=K. Conversely every such S has a count-two realization: spectral S=Σλᵢvᵢvᵢᵀ, K>0, assign measure weights 2λᵢ/K at energy ε=K/2 along vᵢ (or equally ±vᵢ to obtain zero first angular moment). K=0 requires S=0 and arbitrary count-two support at ε=0. This is abstract measure sufficiency; extra eventwise two-electron/recoil momentum correlations impose independent constraints.

For any unit axis a, D=∫ε P₂(a·n)dμ=(3aᵀSa−K)/2. Hence −K/2≤D≤K. Endpoints use count2 at ε=K/2 perpendicular/parallel to a. Any d in this interval is realized by energy-weighted fraction f=(2d/K+1)/3 parallel and 1−f perpendicular; azimuthally average the latter for axial symmetry. In the axisymmetric case S=diag((K−D)/3,(K−D)/3,(K+2D)/3). General symmetric T satisfies −K/3≤λ(T)≤2K/3 and ||T||F≤sqrt(2/3)K. Norm alone is necessary, not sufficient: K=3, T=diag(−1.1,.55,.55) obeys norm bound but S has negative eigenvalue. D bound for one axis alone is also insufficient.

Finite-mass COM energy ledger: A=E−I≥0, electron K plus outgoing proton Kp obey K+Kp=A with Kp≥0. Thus −(A−Kp)/2≤D≤A−Kp. Heavy target Kp=0 is explicitly separate. This event-level formula does not import incoming thermal-energy averages into a pointwise scattering energy. Input E/I/K/Kp units must match; use J in production-facing contracts, exact rationals or any explicitly declared common energy unit in manufactured fixtures.
