## 2. Source measured Li I 1s final-state channels

- Ferrett, Lindle, Heimann, Brewer, Becker, Kerkhoff & Shirley (1987), *Phys. Rev. A* 36, 3172–3183, DOI `10.1103/PhysRevA.36.3172`. **Original scanned Table IV p.3176**: 13 photon energies in [75,99] eV, four partial columns `σ(n=2)`, `σ(n=3)`, `σ(1s2s ^3S)`, and **unresolved** `σ(1s2s ^1S + 1s2p ^3P)`. At E=75,77.5 eV paper does not report `σ(n=3)` and they are left missing. Paper's statistical errors are quoted separately; the total absolute normalization has a further quoted ~20% systematic uncertainty, correlated among channels. The 1s2p ^1P residual is derived `σ(n=2)-σ(^3S)-σ(^1S+^3P)`, **not a newly measured partial channel**.
- At E=85 eV, `(σ_n2,σ_n3,σ_3S,σ_(1S+3P))=(1.58,0.30,0.99,0.53) Mb`. Thus measured n2-subspace `f3S=0.626582...`; `f1S<=0.335443...`, NOT `f1S=0.3354`.
