# C2W general geometry gas owner
Use u=ln a, H>0 and a fixed canonical ray registry. On each supplied interval interpolate F in u with determinant-one normalization and log H linearly; integrate dt=du/H analytically per log-linear segment. This is an interpolation closure, not an Einstein integrator. At knots retain the exact supplied coframe. Canonical angular weights, energy scales and directions replace only the LRS ray geometry; V7 threshold pools, positive N/U states, source, gas Newton, adaptive metric and local energy budgets remain the existing owner.
The external trial takes a fixed endpoint and compares one full and two half steps. The caller's accepted state is not mutated during trial. An interval that changes the accepted starting F or H is rejected. The returned physical pressure is the full symmetric spatial tensor; trace P=J0 and vanishing flux are tested. General-mode checkpoint and LRS-only envelope routines are explicitly unsupported, rather than serializing an incomplete registry.
For the strong LRS comparator use the actual C2V mapped32 gas and source history, frozen in the independent C++ integration. Separate time and frequency axes; nonmonotone threshold aliasing prevents a continuum convergence certificate. The general driver run itself covers z=20 to 19.8 only.
