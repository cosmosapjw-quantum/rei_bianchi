## 2. C5 algorithm actually written
New Rust src/axbi_q_grid.rs / tests/axbi_q_grid.rs / examples/axbi_q_grid_pilot.rs and axbi_q_grid_bench.rs. Represent each FIXED reference-q direction and spectral cell by two f64 arrays: N_ij (photons/H) and U_ij,* (E_ref photons/H). Given E_t=R_j(t) E_* and angular J=(V_ref/V)/R³, the gas-frame source per H and unit time over reference cell is:
dot N_ij = dot N_H(t) ω_j J_j Σ_c f_c/I_c ∫_{[R E_i*,R E_{i+1}*]∩[E_c^-,E_c^+]} shape(E) dE,
dot U_ij,* = dot N_H(t) ω_j J_j/R_j Σ_c f_c/I_c ∫_{clipped} E shape(E) dE,
where shape(E)=E²/expm1(E/k_B Tstar), I_c=∫_{native source cell c} shape(E) dE and f_c is original AxbiSource.photon_fraction. This preserves native source cell photon fractions exactly (up to binary64), and its first moment to GL8-vs-owner-Simpson accuracy; it is an explicit positive within-cell source model, NOT the same bytes as its old 48-energy-centroid representation. J is evaluated using original CharacteristicRay::solid_angle_jacobian, R with its original pullback, angular weight ω from original Gauss-Legendre. No duplicate R or J; each physical spectral intersection is cut at the actual source cell edges including HI,HeI,HeII and 100eV. Whole selected-q support guard E_ref in [13.6,433]eV (sufficient limit432.229372...eV); out-of-bound geometry fails CLOSED and does not commit partial source.

For frozen proper absorbers n_i(cm^-3) the positive centroid surrogate E_ij=R U_ij/N_ij is passed to the actual Verner σ_i with FIT cutoff HI13.60, HeI24.59, HeII54.42eV. Lambda=c Σ n_i σ_i(E), A_i=N(1−exp(−Lambda dt)) Lambda_i/Lambda. After absorption N'=N−ΣA_i; U_ref'=U_ref N'/N. Bind=sum_i A_i I_i with original HHeModel binding energy thresholds distinct from FIT; photoheat Q=sum_i A_i(E−I_i). This is a **two-moment spectral closure**, NOT the continuous blackbody's exact attenuation. The original variable-redshift C3 event-aware frozen-gas solver is reused on each transient cell centroid. Geometry endpoints determine constant effective H_i=log(a_i,1/a_i,0)/dt during a short step; the real AxbiBackground supplies endpoints and proper Δt. No ongoing gas chemistry, recombination or gas-temperature coupling.

Memory O(N_E*N_mu), two fixed state arrays and bounded transactional scratch, unlike new cohorts × all accepted steps. Source, frozen absorber and short transit are parallelized by disjoint angular chunks with 1/2/4 workers; per-cell N,U binary64 equal under serial and four-thread tests. 8-point GL source quadrature at native spectral/source cutoffs is independent of any unbounded packet bank.
