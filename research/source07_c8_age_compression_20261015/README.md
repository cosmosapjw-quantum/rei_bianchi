# SOURCE07 C8 positive source-age sparse diagnostic compression

2026-10-15: standalone executable `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 research/source07_c8_age_compression_20261015/experiment.py`, based on C7’s conditional physical input and the C8 frozen-gas model. 36 manufactured equal-weight source epochs logarithmically spaced from zbar20 to zbar4, q_ref=-0.01,0,+0.01, 12 Gauss angle nodes, threshold-split 16-point energy quadrature, receiver proper gas held fixed, and 1e10s frozen absorption. This is NOT a continuous MD14 total emissivity history, full spectral solver, source emission redshift integration, dynamic chemistry, or validated production source-age compression.

Each age's five-vector contains integrated emitted photon number, received energy, absorbed HI, absorbed HeI, and photoelectron heat. A positive LP basic feasible solution reproduces their 36-epoch weighted sum at the same receiver gas state with five nonzero positive age nodes. Positivity and physical ledgers are conditional on the chosen reaction model. The compacted five-vector is only a chosen receiver functional: a complete energy spectral function, HeII events (initial HeII gas zero), and future absorption as receiver opacity changes are not preserved. Nonnegative weights are numerical and source-age representatives, not proof that a density-independent reduced closure exists.

Measured same-gas max relative errors q=(-.01,0,+.01): (3.41e-12,5.86e-12,2.54e-12). Out-of-sample perturbation nHI -> .6 nHI and nHeI ->1.3 nHeI yields heating relative error ~5.95e-4, HI ~1.01e-6, HeI ~5.35e-6 (q0). This is a constructive failure of transferability at fixed gas-receiver moment closure, not a global impossibility result. If gas changes, require a state-dependent reweighting/error envelope and strict photon/energy/event ledger.
