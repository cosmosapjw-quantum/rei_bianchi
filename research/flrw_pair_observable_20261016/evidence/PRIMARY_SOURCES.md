# Primary-source intake, 2026-10-16

The Exa query searched primary cosmology notes on Thomson optical depth, conformal time and visibility. SciSpace searched optical-depth and visibility expressions and sensitivity to ionization history. These were discovery tools, not numerical inputs. SciSpace bibliographic years/authors were not accepted without primary-source checking.

- Wayne Hu, *Astro 282 lecture notes*: https://background.uchicago.edu/~whu/Courses/Ast282_06/ast282.pdf . Proper-time relation dt=da/(aH); conformal opacity a n_e sigma_T in c=1. Our coordinate conversion gives d tau=c sigma_T n_e dx/H for x=ln a.
- Wayne Hu, *Integral Solutions*: https://background.uchicago.edu/~whu/tamm/webversion/node28.html . Optical depth to the observation endpoint and visibility as opacity times exp(-tau). We use a finite z=20 to 4 interval and a separate no-scattering outcome.
- Holder et al. (2003), *The Reionization History at High Redshifts II*, https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0302404 . Context: equal total optical depth need not imply equal reionization-history effects. No observational threshold or numerical value is imported from this paper.
- NIST, CODATA 2022 complete constants: https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/Table/allascii.txt . Thomson cross section 6.6524587051e-29 m^2, converted to 6.6524587051e-25 cm^2. Standard uncertainty is not interpreted as a deterministic interval. Speed of light 299792458 m/s exact.
