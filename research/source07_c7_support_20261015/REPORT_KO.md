## Source-support connectedness lemma (scope: original MD14 spectrum and selected shear)
For each conserved reference direction mu*, a source injection at a creates reference-energy support I(a,mu*)=[13.6/R(a,mu*),100/R(a,mu*)], where E_current=R E_ref. For the selected expanding family, |s/H|≤0.01, hence positive H_perp=H-s and H_par=H+2s. The redshift R decreases monotonically, and 1/R grows from 1 to no more than (21/5)^(51/50)=4.322... . Thus all source-epoch supports contain the common nonempty interval [13.6 (21/5)^(51/50),100]=approximately[58.7831946356,100] eV. The original blackbody is strictly positive in its emitted [13.6,100] interval. With finite opacity, absorption is positive survival and does not introduce a mathematical zero inside that support. Therefore for this scenario source-age support intervals are connected, and one min/max energy envelope per q bin encloses true source support. This is NOT sufficient to retain its spectral shape or bound nonlinear opacity/heating errors. The result is not a general theorem for narrow-band/multi-peaked sources or underflowing numerical stock.

## Exact age-mixing obstruction
For age components A,B within a single q bin, N=N_A+N_B and U_ref=U_A+U_B are conserved when pooled, but the reaction functional g(E) is nonlinear. With the illustrative smooth manufactured g(E)=E^2, N_A E_A^2+N_B E_B^2-(N_A+N_B)[(N_A E_A+N_B E_B)/(N_A+N_B)]^2 = N_A N_B/(N_A+N_B)(E_A-E_B)^2≥0. Verner cross sections are not globally convex, so this identity explains the mechanism without predicting its sign or magnitude. For accurate HeI/HeII/HI photoevents, positivity of N and U is necessary but not sufficient. Theoretical overlap and mixture identities passed Wolfram, including actual xAct geometry checks.
