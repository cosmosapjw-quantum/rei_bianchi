# Direct DD tritium tracer on the 82 inherited histories

The inherited exact-zero (T^0,T^+) census is an executable choice: zero initial T, no nuclear source or decay variable, and a literal-zero census lift. It is **not** an inference that the listed D(D,p)T reaction is forbidden. The read-only code trace and all-83-file schema audit are in `CODE_PATH_AUDIT.md/json`.

Under the separate, explicitly conditional model in `PHYSICS_CONTRACT.md`, positive D II and positive temperature give a positive DDp source in **every one of the 82 quotient histories**. The model extends the zero-energy bare-ion S factors as constants through the 100–10,000 K Maxwellian tail. Its Gamow peaks are only 2.635–56.769 eV, below the cited keV experimental regime. Thus these are model calculations, not experimentally validated low-temperature rates or a full nuclear-network result. The source is retained in natural logarithms even where ordinary floating point would underflow.

The largest final direct-T birth yield is (C_p=3.96127\times10^{-96}) per baryon, while the surviving direct-T yield after the evaluated 12.312-year half-life is (Y_T=1.39375\times10^{-104}) per baryon. The companion DDn channel has (C_n=3.97687\times10^{-96}) neutron births per baryon. The strongest case is `quotient_L16_S0.05_Am1.6_Ar1.6`; the endpoint direct-T birth range over all cases is log₁₀ (C_p=-95.402332) to (-95.402166). The first-generation, no-decay, no-recycling secondary-T counting ceiling is (C_n), and the combined primary-plus-secondary ceiling is (C_p+C_n) under those assumptions. Energetic neutron slowing/capture, triton stopping, nuclear feedback, and neutral/screened pathways were not calculated.

The event formula uses (s_c=\tfrac12 n_B(Y_D f_{DII})^2 k_c) per baryon per second with SI (k_c). The 1/2 counts identical D reactants. DDp conserves A and Z as (2D\to T+p); DDn as (2D\to{}^3He+n). The reported maximum passive D-depletion fraction (2(C_p+C_n)/Y_D=8.412\times10^{-91}) is below the contract's (10^{-6}) threshold. The all-D comparator in the saved NPZ uses the **same bare-ion kernel** and is not a bound on actual neutral or screened reactions.

The source is interpolated linearly in saved proper time and convolved exactly on each interval with tritium decay. A constant-source transient control agrees with its analytic solution to (2.885\times10^{-14}) relative. Independent quadrature tightening at 100, 1000, and 10000 K changes log rate by less than (10^{-7}) (observed zero at output precision). A 2× reconstructed-grid sweep changes cumulative births by at most (1.744\times10^{-5}) dex, below the 0.02-dex acceptance; no optional 4× run was needed. The refinement tests the interpolation of an observed background, not its unknown subgrid truth. The local quasi-steady (s_p/\lambda_T) curve is labeled separately from exact piecewise-linear-source survival.

`TRACER_HISTORIES.npz` contains all 82 case names, backgrounds needed for review, natural-log rates/sources, cumulative births, survival, quasi-steady comparison, and all-D comparator. `TRACER_REPORT.json` contains numeric checks and endpoint ranges. `01_DD_source.png` and `02_births_vs_survival.png` visualize the strongest case. The inherited 83 IVPs and figures were not rerun or rewritten, and no production default was changed. Scientific admission of these conditional results belongs to independent review; no 99% confidence or full 19-channel claim follows.
