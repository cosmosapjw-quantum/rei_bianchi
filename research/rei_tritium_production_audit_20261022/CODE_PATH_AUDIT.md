# Tritium production code-path audit

The inherited pilot's plotted (T^0=T^+=0) is **structural**, not a nuclear-rate prediction. `pilot/code/atomic_quotient.py:23–27` initializes elemental T at zero; `:142–150` writes literal zeros for its two census charge states at every sample. The nine-dimensional chemistry in `:38–45,119–140` evolves shared H/He/Li atomic charge fractions only. `pilot/code/run.py:52–95` integrates transport, those conditional fractions, and optical depth, with no `D(D,p)T` source or T-decay equation. Its reference mode has 18 duplicated atomic conditional variables and likewise no T state (`atomic_quotient.py:152–176`). The inherited channel matrix expressly marks reaction 14 unimplemented and capable of making T from zero initial inventory (`intake/NUCLEAR19_APPLICABILITY.md:22–35`).

All 83 saved NPZ files were inspected read-only: 82 quotient histories and one full-reference history. Each has 257 samples; `x(257)`, `state(257,49)`, `conditional(257,9)`, `census(257,21)`, and `diagnostics(257,37)`. Both census T columns are identically zero for all cases. The saved `census_names` expose `D_0`, `D_1`, `T_0`, `T_1`; `diagnostic_names` expose `H_per_s`, `nB_m3`, `T_K`, and `elapsed_s`. Relevant arrays are finite. Across the full set, (H=6.6222\times10^{-18}\) to (1.2207\times10^{-16}\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}), (n_B=6.8413) to (2346.56\,\mathrm{m}^{-3}), and the prescribed gas temperature is 100.00002 to 9998.51 K. Both D charge columns have positive samples. The `abundances` NPZ member is an object array and need not be loaded; `numpy.load(..., allow_pickle=False)` suffices for every required field.

A bounded offline tracer can evaluate a source on each frozen history. The frozen conditional bare-ion model selects `D_1`: in SI, \(s_{DDp}=nB\_m3(D_1)^2\langle\sigma v\rangle/2\) per baryon per second, with \(\langle\sigma v\rangle\) in m³/s. The factor \(1/2\) counts identical D pairs. Its all-D same-kernel comparator substitutes `D_0+D_1`; this does not bound real neutral or screened channels. The saved elapsed time permits decay postprocessing under the frozen physical contract’s lifetime and interpolation convention. An offline result is a tracer on frozen atomic and cosmological histories; it does not include depletion feedback, nuclear heat, or the rest of the 19-channel network.

Source-byte identifiers and the exact inspection recipe are in [CODE_PATH_AUDIT.json](CODE_PATH_AUDIT.json). The old results and production code were not modified. The old two-profile source-hash exception is documented in `pilot/code/run.py:226–240`; this audit records current source identity and does not rehash the result corpus.
