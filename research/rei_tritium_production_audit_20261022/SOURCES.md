## S1 — Coherent two-channel zero-energy theoretical input

M. Viviani, L. Girlanda, A. Kievsky, D. Logoteta, L. E. Marcucci, *Study of the d(d,p)3H and d(d,n)3He processes at low energies*, Journal of Physics: Conference Series 2586 (2023) 012076. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/2586/1/012076. Primary paper fetched through https://inspirehep.net/files/fc3a3fe08ab5d1beaa365ec51e2c7565 (four-page PDF; search-service result `turn35view1`). Exa independently surfaced the same paper.

One short quotation, p.3: “S(0) = 50.8±1.9 keV b [51.0±1.4 keV b].”

The first value is DDp and the bracketed value DDn. The work solves few-body scattering with hyperspherical harmonics and chiral two-/three-nucleon interactions. Its bands compare 500 and 600 MeV interaction cutoffs. The authors call their uncertainty assessment preliminary and propose further assessment; these widths are not an empirical confidence interval. We use the positive zero-energy central values as constants in an explicitly conditional low-energy cross-section continuation. The paper does not validate that constant model, plasma screening, or Maxwellian distributions in this cosmological history. Its plotted finite-energy data do not cover the audit's eV-scale Gamow window.
