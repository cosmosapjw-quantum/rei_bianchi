# IGM heat/range repair contract

- Task type: numerical defect repair and bounded continuation.
- Observable outcome: accept a finite, certified-positive subnormal photoheat term from the saved coarse k11 cell, without changing source, provider, closure, tolerances, IGM default, or stored historical evidence.
- Preserved invariants: common A/B spectrum event, threshold identity and units; negative or sign-uncertain heat rejection; atomic rejected state; N/E and original 37-field gates; all prior costs and failures.
- First implementation scope: local heat difference and its binary64 arithmetic bound; regression tests; one next coarse/fine/tail step from existing checkpoints.
- Conditional second scope: one local repair only if that bounded step exposes the already-audited paired N/E representation boundary.
- Out of scope: rerunning completed 190-step or 0.0004 work, source/provider/closure/tolerance changes, donor recomputation, physical admission, merge.
- Completion bar: targeted tests and locked library tests pass; saved heat witness passes; next common state either passes all original gates or preserves a new first failure; commands/costs/review/remaining blockers are recorded.

Claim ceiling remains `HOLD`: a local numerical repair or one common-step pass is not z=12→10 completion or continuum validation.
