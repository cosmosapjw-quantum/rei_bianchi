# Source acknowledgments

The coefficient data are scientific fit parameters from the following original
authors. Their downloaded bytes, headers and metadata are retained without
claiming a new license over them.

- D. A. Verner, G. J. Ferland, K. T. Korista and D. G. Yakovlev (1996),
  *Atomic Data for Astrophysics. II*, ApJ 465, 487,
  DOI <https://doi.org/10.1086/177435>.
- G. S. Voronov (1997), *A Practical Fit Formula for Ionization Rate
  Coefficients of Atoms and Ions by Electron Impact: Z = 1–28*, ADNDT 65, 1–35,
  DOI <https://doi.org/10.1006/adnd.1997.0732>.
- D. A. Verner and G. J. Ferland (1996), *Atomic Data for Astrophysics. I*,
  ApJS 103, 467–473, DOI <https://doi.org/10.1086/192284>.
- D. A. Verner and D. G. Yakovlev (1995), A&AS 109, 125, is additionally
  acknowledged for the inner-shell parameters retained in the complete original
  `phfit2.f` reference; this bounded catalog uses the 1996 outer-shell table.

Original site: <https://www.pa.uky.edu/~verner/>. Its `info.html` asks users to
acknowledge the server in publications. The retrieved pages and Fortran headers
do not provide an explicit software/data redistribution license. This record
does not designate those external files as MIT, public domain, or any other
license. The Rust and Python implementation in this subunit is a fresh expression
of the published formulas; the complete original Fortran files serve only as
archived coefficient and formula references, not linked executable dependencies.

There is no runtime dependency on SciSpace, Exa, Wolfram, Python packages, or
network access. Their research receipts are provenance records. Citations and
atomic fit validity remain applicable when generated Rust parameters are reused.
