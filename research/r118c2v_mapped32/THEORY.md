# C2V mapped angular refinement and general coframe source adapter

For invertible oriented spatial coframes C_0 and C, fix canonical momentum covectors. The physical ray momentum is p=C^{-T} C_0^T n_0. The map to current direction is p/|p|, energy scale R=|p|, and angular Jacobian det(C_0)/det(C)/R^3. For an arbitrary fixed center coframe, first map Gauss-Legendre/azimuth nodes from center to reference; multiply positive weights by the exact Jacobian. Then map the reference rays to each evaluation coframe. Normalize only source weights; transported stocks own their reference energy and are never multiplied by a second source weight. The physical pressure moment is the sum of transported energies times n_i n_j. Its trace equals energy by the unit-sphere identity. This construction does not require simultaneous diagonalization.

Tests evaluate isotropic birth weights, raw area, dipole and STF pressure through the actual C2V BASS geometry history and the strong prescribed LRS path. The centered grid gives improved worst-case moments on the latter. Center choice is an illustrative history-dependent fixed choice, not an optimality proof. This is a separate adapter, not a general coframe coupled gas owner.

Fresh mapped32 uses exactly the C2U mapped executable and physical tolerances (r=.5, 35 energy cells, strong LRS). Same-identity restarts preserve every state. The full mapped16/mapped32 comparison uses common redshift samples; the frozen-gas C++ result is an independent conditional comparator, not an exact coupled reference. No frequency/time convergence claim follows.
