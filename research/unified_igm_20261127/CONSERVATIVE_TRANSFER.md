## Number and energy equations

Write dN=S dξ for species counts. For chemical/electronic reactions with no nuclear transmutation, the H- and He-nucleus rows of S separately vanish after contraction with their respective inventories. The total-charge row also vanishes when swept charged CRs and external currents, if present, are included consistently. For e.g. pure hydrogen photoionization N_HI→N_HI−1, N_HII→N_HII+1, N_e→N_e+1. Per-baryon Y=N/N_b has derivative S V R/N_b for conserved N_b. The neutral H and He reference states have zero ionization energy; ion storage is B=χ_H N_HII+χ_HeI N_HeII+(χ_HeI+χ_HeII) N_HeIII. Extend this inventory only with known species and binding references.

Let U_g be gas thermal energy exclusive of B, U_exc energy temporarily stored in atomic excitation and U_γ,U_CR the respective radiation and CR energies (kinetic CR energy when species rest inventories are separately controlled). Homogeneous comoving energy conservation is

    d(U_g+B+U_exc+U_γ+U_CR) + V Σᵢ Hᵢ P_ii dτ = dE_in − dE_escape.

Pressure is the total local physical stress in the comoving tetrad. This expression assumes geodesic comoving observers with no spatial flux divergence. Rest energy must also be accounted for if a reaction changes the sum of tracked rest masses beyond the separately represented binding term. For a gas-only collision substep at frozen geometry the pressure-work increment is zero. Escaped radiation is either a boundary flux or a cumulative escaped stock; the latter is added only once. Algebraic OTS deposition and explicit propagation of the same trapped photon are mutually exclusive for that branch of the event; an explicit survivor reservoir can subsequently reabsorb on a different clock. At fixed V a monatomic ideal mixture has 3k_B T N_part/2=U_g. The exact finite-step temperature change is [2ΔU_g/(3k_B)−TΔN_part]/[N_part+ΔN_part], provided the denominator is positive. It includes the change of heat capacity as ionization produces particles.

For CRs with conserved covariant momenta in Bianchi I, physical tetrad momenta satisfy dp_i/dτ=−H_i p_i. With T=√(p²c²+m²c⁴)−mc², vᵢ=c²pᵢ/(T+mc²), this gives dT/dτ=−ΣHᵢpᵢvᵢ. In FLRW this simplifies to −HT(T+2mc²)/(T+mc²). The asymptotic loss coefficients are 2H for nonrelativistic kinetic energy and H for ultrarelativistic kinetic energy. Summing the single-particle relation over the comoving cell reproduces its part of the pressure-work identity. Explicit transport in momentum space accounts for that work; the gas and CR equations must consume disjoint budget increments.

A second example determines when energy can safely be compressed. Let N≥0 be a photon-bin number vector, with bin energies E. For a fixed linear response k·N to depend only on (1·N,E·N) for every nonnegative N, it is necessary and sufficient that k∈span{1,E}. Sufficiency follows by substitution. For necessity choose a strictly positive N₀ and any h⊥1,E; for sufficiently small ε both N₀±εh lie in the positive cone and have equal specified moments. Equal response then gives k·h=0. Taking every h shows the row-span condition. More moment constraints give the corresponding row space. For E=(10,20,30)eV choose A=(0,1,0), B=(1/2,0,1/2). Both have number 1 and energy20eV. For k=1[E≥13.6](13.6/E)^3, the responses are 4913/15625 and 19652/421875; their ratio is 4/27. Exact Python rational arithmetic recovered both values. The threshold model is an explicitly prescribed example. No atomic cross-section fit is inferred from it. The result applies to unknown unrestricted discrete spectral shape; a validated restricted family or a preintegrated rate supplies additional information. This is a computational interface criterion, with no claim of a new impossibility theorem.

A corresponding discretization of pure losses on a kinetic-energy grid reconciles accounts through summation by parts. If F_j denotes the signed particle flux through face j in the increasing energy direction, then dN_k/dτ = F_{k−1/2}−F_{k+1/2}+S_k. For fixed representative kinetic energies E_k, the exact discrete energy derivative is ΣE_k S_k + E_1F_{1/2}−E_KF_{K+1/2}+Σ_{k<K}(E_{k+1}−E_k)F_{k+1/2}. Finite-volume continuous-loss deposition therefore consumes the measured energy removed including separately attributed boundary and adiabatic terms. On a moving grid, include Σ(dE_k/dτ)N_k; when a reconstructed cell energy is evolved independently, use its own conservative energy equation. Per-atom ionization and thermal deposition require the separate species-resolved loss integral or the validated representation of the spectrum. Number or energy alone leave that response underdetermined.
