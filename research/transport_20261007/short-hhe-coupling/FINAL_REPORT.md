## Review scope limitations

The 2,440-node bound establishes the number of distinct spectral abscissae. Multiple old, candidate and finite-difference density arrays coexist; the independent Radau solver also retains node-by-time arrays. The original “live samples” wording does not explicitly resolve that distinction. Resource acceptance is therefore conditional on the distinct-node interpretation. Under a strict simultaneous stored-spectral-values interpretation the cap is exceeded: old and returned Gauss4 density arrays alone coexist with 4,880 values, and Newton retains additional base/perturbed arrays. No unconditional claim of the live-sample cap passing is made.

The exact event anchor and continuity checks apply within each photon transaction. Between coupled transactions, the state retains per-node counts and aggregate radiation energy, then reconstructs per-node start energies from eta. The original global energy budget independently admits the resulting roundoff, but a separate per-node cross-transaction energy-continuity test is absent. This limits exact-continuity claims; it is not evidence that the observed first-order temporal error is a ledger-bookkeeping defect. A future contract should explicitly choose and test the carried energy convention.
