### Key mathematical separation

At a fixed eta, splitting the mu0 integral at the analytically known support boundary avoids integrating a cross-section jump as though it were smooth. This is a quadrature representation of the same fixed-momentum measure dmu0, not a second direction Jacobian. But the optical depth at a newly selected direction contains its entire earlier path. Moving nodes alone do not reconstruct that history. A proposal must either retain fixed rays, evaluate their histories through a separately controlled representation, or introduce an explicitly bounded approximation. A conservation ledger does not by itself bound the resulting Li fractional error.

The obvious wrong-reference controls are to integrate a step with unpartitioned low-order quadrature, omit the moving support cut, or change number weights without the corresponding history/energy mapping. Such tests should be local and finite. Existing smooth U_gamma/Q convergence is insufficient to certify threshold chemistry.
