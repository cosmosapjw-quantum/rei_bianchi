## Primary literature and tool evidence

Switzer and Hirata, Primordial helium recombination I: feedback, line transfer, and continuum opacity, Phys. Rev. D 77, 083006 (2008), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.77.083006, arXiv:astro-ph/0702143 (first submitted 2007-02-06). The official arXiv abstract provides bibliographic identity. Exa retrieved the original introduction and beginning of section II; targeted search excerpts also identified section II.2's particle-number correction. SciSpace independently located the journal record. The HTML rendering date is not the original publication date.

The directly read introduction explains that the recombination binding-energy release predominantly goes into photons in that recombination setting and that matter temperature must track both translational energy and the changing number of translational degrees of freedom. This supports separating atomic binding from gas heat and retaining the number correction. The paper's energy per degree of freedom, k_B T/2, is not the energy per monatomic particle, 3 k_B T/2. This source does not validate a general nonthermal bath, arbitrary Bianchi transport, or the absent recipient EOS. The finite maps below are direct derivations under explicit assumptions, not a claimed new result of that paper.
