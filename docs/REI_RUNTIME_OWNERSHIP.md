# REI independent runtime and REC initial condition (R118-C2G)

The existing AXBI solver owns its H/He fractions, gas thermal variable, photon groups, source injection, absorption and collision ledgers. This is not a new fitted xe history. AX01 and historical AX08 conditional admissions are unchanged. The primary-only homogeneous source, non-tilted LRS Bianchi I geometry and finite energy/angular closures remain assumptions.

The two legacy compile-time fixtures were restored byte-for-byte from fef2baf960f5. The paired_history, first_interval, flrw_three_probe and other existing examples compile/test; this does not admit their previously withheld F05/AX11 physics. The active worktree retains other untracked historical fixtures.

DriverConfig accepts explicit initial_x_hii, initial_x_heii, initial_x_heiii and initial_state_id. Original defaults remain unchanged. REC_HMLA_<donor SHA256> denotes the new typed boundary, generated by REC's independent HMLA+Tm solver under the matching REI AX01 H/4He inventory. run_owned.py validates donor scenario, inventory, initial values and provenance. No later donor samples are used by the REI integrator.

Build with cargo build --release --locked --offline --manifest-path rust/rei_microphysics/Cargo.toml --example axbi_reionization. Reproduce 12 short lanes with research/r118c2g_owned_history/run_owned.py and the exact REC donor (normal JSON), scenario and executable. For the single new full z20->4 FLRW lane use the committed REC_q0_long.cfg:
  axbi_reionization --config REC_q0_long.cfg --output NEW_DIRECTORY --case REC_q0_long
The receipt records original actual output directory and hashes. audit_long.py independently reads all history and ledger rows. Raw outputs remain on the workstation. The long lane uses reduced angular/energy resolution and local_target=8e-5: it is an execution result, not a new convergence/admission of the full AX08 family. No general-Bianchi tilted chemistry or active BASS feedback. No z<4 forced tail.
