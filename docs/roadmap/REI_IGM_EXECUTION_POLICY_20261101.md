Keep the complete failure history, but combine routine R/C stage receipts into a single replayable evidence ledger. If the user explicitly requests a backup at each stage, record each stage atomically; batching cloud transport is acceptable only when all requested checkpoints remain individually identifiable and recoverable.
2. **After two auxiliary-only units**, state the central edge they enable, the measured dependency defect, and the smallest falsification of that defect; otherwise choose the central critical path next. This is a **focus prompt**, not a mandatory approval/CI gate. User-directed exploration may continue.
3. **No automatic elevation.** Replaying frozen chemistry, finding source photon cross sections, a successful CI or a numerically sharp `N,M` moment bound **does not** complete a full evolving IGM/Bianchi node. Explicit physical acceptance and provenance drive canonical changes.
4. **Scope locks stay scoped.** The F4 `NO_MORE_IVPS` prohibits unapproved IVPs in that F4 campaign, not the separately authorized *central* first-canonical-interval continuation; conversely it does not grant permission to new central production runs. Keep `NO_MERGE`, `NO_FORCE_PUSH`, `PRODUCTION=HOLD`, no reset of compute budgets, and preserve current immutable parent owners.
5. **Finite adversarial review.** One physical/numerical self-review and at most one risk-justified independent review per newly changed scientific claim. Fix concrete defects; do not audit the audit. Distinct-author checks remain NOT_RUN until actually dispatched; Wolfram symbolic checks, same-author alternative integration and GitHub CI are not substitutes.
6. **Conserve evidence, not administrative entropy.** Exact hash for immutable inputs; numerical residual/observable for scientific equivalence; one reproducible source-inclusive private archive when appropriate, and public source-free report/code as allowed. Do not claim dual backup until two separate provider acknowledgements and selective restore/hash evidence exist. Do not delay core work over optional archival formatting once source safety is secured.
