# Claim–source audit

## C-001 — `C` is an autonomous physical state

**Status: UNSUPPORTED / CONTRADICTED BY ITS DEFINITION.**

`C=N_HI,start/Delta t+R_rec` changes when the numerical interval changes at
fixed physical state.  No independent microscopic evolution equation for `C`
exists in the locked model.  The original source calls it a necessary
whole-interval storage-plus-maintenance capacity.

## C-002 — `J_g` is a material reservoir that may be relaxed independently

**Status: UNSUPPORTED.**

The inherited node quantity is an absorption current.  C2-Ray obtains the
photoionization rate from absorbed photons and a time-averaged neutral/opacity
state.  A photon-number store would be a distinct radiation variable; this
stage does not contain one at node level.

## C-003 — R2C-R1’s 497 Farkas certificates prove no physical history exists

**Status: MISATTRIBUTED / TOO STRONG.**

They rigorously prove infeasibility of the *common-equilibrium box model*.
They do not survive a change to the correct state space.  The 491 radiative
certificates use non-state coordinates; the six mass certificates require an
extrapolated equilibrium outside the cap despite feasible endpoints and a
feasible convex segment.

## C-004 — A positive H chemistry operator preserves `0<=x_HII<=1`

**Status: SUPPORTED AND PROVED.**

The two-state H generator is Metzler with zero column sums.  Boundary vector
fields point into the nonnegative orthant, and nonnegative transfer sources
preserve positivity.

## C-005 — The structural blocker is resolved

**Status: SUPPORTED WITH A NARROW SCOPE.**

The claim being resolved is the interpretation of the R2C-R1 Farkas result as
a physical no-go.  It is not a claim that a unique interior RT/thermal history
has already been obtained.  Production authorization remains false.
