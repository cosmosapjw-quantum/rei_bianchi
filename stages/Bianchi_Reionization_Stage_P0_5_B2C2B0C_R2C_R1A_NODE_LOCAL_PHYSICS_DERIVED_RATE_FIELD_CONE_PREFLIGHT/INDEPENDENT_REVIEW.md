# Independent review and adversarial verdict

## What the stage establishes

1. The original storage-plus-recombination expression is a necessary
   whole-interval photon budget. It is preserved in cumulative form rather
   than discarded.
2. Its promotion to a node-local autonomous state is contradicted by its
   explicit `Delta t` dependence and by the absence of an independent
   microscopic evolution law.
3. The inherited `J_g` fields are absorption/photoionization fluxes. A true
   radiation reservoir would require an explicit photon-number state and
   transport equation, neither of which is present in the tested surrogate.
4. All inherited material endpoints and their affine segments pass the
   state/mass-cap cone. Therefore an extrapolated equilibrium failure is not
   an endpoint-path no-go.
5. The positive H reaction generator and cumulative photon identity provide a
   physically meaningful replacement taxonomy.

## Strongest objections and responses

### Objection 1 — the old capacity gate was physically motivated

Correct. It remains a valid necessary bound on **integrated** photoionizations
over the specified interval. The stage rejects only its use as an independently
relaxed pointwise coordinate and the node-wise mass-proportional allocation
derived from that use.

### Objection 2 — a straight endpoint segment is not a physical RT trajectory

Correct. The convex segment is only a counterexample to the claim that the
endpoints are separated by the state/mass cone. It proves that the six mass
Farkas rows are caused by common-equilibrium extrapolation. R2C-R1B must still
construct a photon-conserving nonautonomous trajectory.

### Objection 3 — `J_g=Gamma_g N_HI` may be too local in optically thick cells

Correct. In a finite photon-conserving cell, the precise relation involves
absorbed photon counts and time-averaged neutral population/opacity. The stage
uses `J_g=Gamma_g N` as instantaneous notation for the same reaction flux and
explicitly defers the finite-cell time-average closure to R2C-R1B.

### Objection 4 — the direct collisional/recombination formulae are incomplete

They are diagnostic rate evidence only. The blocker resolution follows from
state taxonomy, positivity, dimensional/refinement covariance, endpoint
signs, and the exact cumulative identity; it does not rely on promoting these
particular coefficient fits to production atomic physics.

### Objection 5 — resolving a category error does not resolve the project

Agreed. It resolves the named R2C-R1 blocker, not the full node-history problem.
Production authorization remains false.

## Red-team alternatives

- **Alternative A: hidden unresolved photon reservoir.** Possible, but it would
  require an explicit radiation storage/transport state. It cannot be
  represented by independently relaxing the old `C` budget.
- **Alternative B: node-local free rates.** Rejected as underidentified and
  capable of fitting arbitrary endpoints while hiding the category error.
- **Alternative C: immediate large coupled generator.** Held in reserve. The
  minimal corrected nonautonomous RT/chemistry closure has not yet failed.
