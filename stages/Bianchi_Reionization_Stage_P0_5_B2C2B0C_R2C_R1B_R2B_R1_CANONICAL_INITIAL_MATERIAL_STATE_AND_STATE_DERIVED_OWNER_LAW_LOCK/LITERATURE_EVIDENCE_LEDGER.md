Used for the rule that removed photons must be assigned exactly once to the material process that owns the absorption.

- **Self-shielding auditor:** Rahmati et al., *On the evolution of the H I column density distribution in cosmological simulations*, MNRAS 430 (2013), arXiv:1210.7808. Used only for the non-production SCRIPT/self-shielding auditor lane.
