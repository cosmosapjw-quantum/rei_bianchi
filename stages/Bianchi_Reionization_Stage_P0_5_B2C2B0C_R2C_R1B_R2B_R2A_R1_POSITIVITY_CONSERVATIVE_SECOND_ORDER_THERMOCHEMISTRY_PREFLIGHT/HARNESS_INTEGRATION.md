# Harness integration

The research harness supplied the evidence/claim split, competing hypotheses,
adversarial no-go test and bounded promotion policy.  It prevented the failed
trapezoidal method from being silently replaced without an attempt-specific
precalculation lock and exposed the event-level PDS identifiability limit.

The coding harness supplied RED--GREEN cycles for PDS decomposition, MPRK22,
thermal candidates, integrated trial, exact validation and performance roots.
Failed benchmark and validator attempts are retained rather than overwritten.
