# Literature evidence ledger

1. **Friedrich et al. 2012, arXiv:1201.0602.** Primary source for coupled full-OTS H/He branching, multifrequency ionization/heating, and the statement that temperature convergence can be more restrictive than ionization convergence. Load-bearing for the event graph and for the branch-kernel no-go.
2. **Mellema et al. 2006, C2-Ray.** Primary method source for photon-conserving cell absorption and time-averaged opacity iteration. Used only as background consistency.
3. **Hummer & Seaton 1964.** Source cited by Friedrich et al. for the temperature-dependent two-photon branch `v(T)`. The numerical table/function is not present in the repository, which is a load-bearing absence.
4. **Flower & Perinotto 1980.** Source cited by Friedrich et al. for He III to He II cascade treatment. Supports the channel taxonomy, not the project sigmoid/exponential closures.
5. **Ali-Haimoud & Hirata 2011, HyRec.** Shows that high-accuracy primordial recombination evolves radiation and populations together. Monitored as an external project only.
6. **Ellis & van Elst 1998, arXiv:gr-qc/9812046.** Standard 1+3 covariant fluid/kinetic framework used to lock the energy-projection interface.
7. **Challinor 1999 and Challinor–Lasenby 1998.** Covariant CMB Boltzmann/Thomson framework used to lock the future optical-depth interface.
8. **Chan et al. 2019, arXiv:1901.04581.** Covariant polarized radiative transfer derived from phase-space and photon-number conservation; supports preserving tetrad-covariant source interfaces.
