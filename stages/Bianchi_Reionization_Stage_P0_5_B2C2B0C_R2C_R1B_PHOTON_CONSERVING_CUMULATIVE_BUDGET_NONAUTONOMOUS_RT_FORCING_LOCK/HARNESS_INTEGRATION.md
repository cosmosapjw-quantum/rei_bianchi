The GPT-5.6 phys-math research harness was applied as a full bounded loop:

1. research contract and completion bar;
2. broad and targeted primary-literature evidence acquisition;
3. claim-source audit;
4. predeclared H1--H4 hypothesis graph;
5. independent adversarial review;
6. exact physics/mathematics validation;
7. minimal decisive verification design;
8. external decision gate;
9. survivor-only formalization;
10. closeout and durable compaction.

The harness archive was hash recorded and its workspace validator passed.
The coding harness was not applied.  Analysis scripts are evidence generators,
not a production implementation.
