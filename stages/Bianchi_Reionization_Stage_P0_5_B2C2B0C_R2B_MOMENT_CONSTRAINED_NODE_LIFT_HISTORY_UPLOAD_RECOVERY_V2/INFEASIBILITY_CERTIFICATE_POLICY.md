# R2B fail-closed policy

If total node cycling capacity is smaller than assigned group current, if a positive target has zero prior support, or if the nonlinear dual solve misses its column/KKT tolerances, the case must stop with a dual and violated-constraint certificate. No clipping, support injection, cloud-mass inversion, or post-hoc renormalization is permitted. No such infeasibility occurred in the accepted 540 cases.
