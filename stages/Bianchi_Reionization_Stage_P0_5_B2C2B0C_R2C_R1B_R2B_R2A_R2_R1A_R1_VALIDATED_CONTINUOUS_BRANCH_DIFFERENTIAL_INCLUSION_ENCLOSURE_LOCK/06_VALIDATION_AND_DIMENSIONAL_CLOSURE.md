# Validation and dimensional closure

Reduced ionization coordinates and branch probabilities are dimensionless; `log T` is dimensionless relative to kelvin input.  The RHS has units `s^-1`.  PCHIP bounds use seconds, reaction rates use `cm^3 s^-1`, populations use `cMpc^-3`, and thermal energy uses erg.  The H and He nuclei identities are structural in the coordinate map.
