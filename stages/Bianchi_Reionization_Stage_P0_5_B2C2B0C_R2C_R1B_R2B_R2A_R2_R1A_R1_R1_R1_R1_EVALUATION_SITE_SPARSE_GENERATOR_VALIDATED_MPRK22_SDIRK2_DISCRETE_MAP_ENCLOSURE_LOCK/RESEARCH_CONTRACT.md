controlled nonlinear composition lacks an outward remainder and event-aware

## Competing hypotheses

1. **H1 — primal wrapper drift:** making four sites explicit changes the
   thermochemistry map.
2. **H2 — local population obstruction:** an H or He implicit block is singular
   or fails interval inclusion over the source-safe corner hull.
3. **H3 — local thermal obstruction:** an SDIRK root derivative can contain zero
   or its Krawczyk image escapes the local tube.

   encloses the allowed stagewise family.
5. **H5 — composition obstruction:** local solves are regular, while the
   independent-site state feedback, topology events and global ledgers lack a
   validated nonlinear remainder.

## Decision

H1–H3 are falsified by exact parity and the local certificates. H4 is falsified
by the fresh upper-to-lower schedule witness. H5 survives and is the only
load-bearing blocker carried forward.

24 inherited trajectories, the stagewise witness and interior falsification
samples while closing topology restart and set-valued ledgers.
