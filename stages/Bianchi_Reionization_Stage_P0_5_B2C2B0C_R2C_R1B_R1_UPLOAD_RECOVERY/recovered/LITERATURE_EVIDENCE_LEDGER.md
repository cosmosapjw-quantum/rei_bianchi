# Literature evidence ledger

The web-assisted research loop was restricted to primary or original technical sources and used only to test architecture, not to replace canonical repository evidence.

1. Mellema et al., *C2-Ray: A new method for photon-conserving transport of ionizing radiation*, New Astronomy 11 (2006), arXiv:astro-ph/0508416. Load-bearing use: separate photon-conserving absorbed counts from chemistry state and iterate RT with time-averaged opacity rather than fitting endpoint rates.
2. Friedrich et al., *Radiative transfer of energetic photons: X-rays and helium ionization in C2-Ray*, MNRAS 421 (2012), arXiv:1201.0602. Load-bearing use: keep multifrequency H/He chemistry and thermal convergence as separate gates.
3. Verner, Ferland, Korista & Yakovlev, *Atomic data for astrophysics. II. New analytic fits for photoionization cross sections of atoms and ions*, ApJ 465 (1996) 487. Load-bearing use: analytic H I, He I and He II photoionization cross sections with exact thresholds.
4. Rahmati et al., *On the evolution of the H I column density distribution in cosmological simulations*, MNRAS 430 (2013) 2427. Non-load-bearing adversarial use: self-shielding fits are useful empirical comparators but are not promoted to the canonical node allocation law.
5. The project’s pinned SCRIPT/FlexRT/C2-Ray source receipts. Load-bearing use: preserve ownership of source, boundary transport, opacity, and sink terms and forbid double counting.

No literature source is used to invent missing source/\(f_{\rm esc}\) histories or to override the B2C2A-R1 dense trajectory.
