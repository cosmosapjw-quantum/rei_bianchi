# Research contract

## Question
Can the fixed R2B node endpoints be connected, for all three shape priors and all ten reduced-DAE substeps, by positive rate families derived from inherited secants and explicit physical process rates while preserving every locked macro/group/global moment and the node physical cone?

## Primary hypothesis
At least one macro-shared one-mode rate vector exists inside the prelocked physical/nuisance intervals for every lane/substep/macro, and its complete analytic trajectory remains in the cone.

## Adversarial alternatives
1. Endpoint and rate-box constraints are already linearly infeasible; a Farkas ray identifies the missing physical degree of freedom.
2. A physical equilibrium exists, but differential rate families create an interior-time `I>M` or `J_G1+J_G2a>C` violation.
3. A one-mode path fails but a bounded two-mode completely monotone path repairs only the interior trajectory, not an equilibrium infeasibility.

## Success criterion
All 540 macro cases pass identifiability, equilibrium LP/KKT, analytic cone certification, exact endpoint moments and `dt/2,dt/4,dt/8` refinement. Only then may R2C-R2 be authorized.

## Stop conditions
Any unidentifiable rate needed to move a nonconstant endpoint, any infeasible LP, any uncertified interior cone interval, any exact-ledger failure, or any use of clipping closes the stage without production authorization.
