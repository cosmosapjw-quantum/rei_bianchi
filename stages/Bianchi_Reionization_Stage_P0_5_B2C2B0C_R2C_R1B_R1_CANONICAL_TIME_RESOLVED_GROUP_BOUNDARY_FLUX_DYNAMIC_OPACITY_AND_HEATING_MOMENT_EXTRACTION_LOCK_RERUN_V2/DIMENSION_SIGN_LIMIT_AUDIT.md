# Dimension, sign and limit audit

- `n_s sigma_sg L` is dimensionless optical depth.
- `h=W tau` and `q=h/sum h` are dimensionless nonnegative measures.
- `kappa_i=kappa_global q_i` has `cMpc^-1`.
- `J_i=J_global q_i` has `s^-1 cMpc^-3`; `J/kappa` has `s^-1 cMpc^-2`.
- Thermal terms have `erg cm^-3 s^-1`.
- Unsupported species/group pairs and primary G3 source occupation are exact zero.
- `tau -> 0` gives sigma-weighted thin heating; `tau -> infinity` gives the thick group moment.
- No sign clipping or opacity/cloud-mass inversion is used.
