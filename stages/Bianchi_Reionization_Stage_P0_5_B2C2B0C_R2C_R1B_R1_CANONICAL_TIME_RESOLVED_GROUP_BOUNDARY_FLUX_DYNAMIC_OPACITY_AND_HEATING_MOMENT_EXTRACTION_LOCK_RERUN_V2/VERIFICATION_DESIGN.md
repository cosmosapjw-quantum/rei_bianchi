# Verification design

- BDF replay: endpoint/state/ledger comparison against inherited canonical tables.
- Time representation: global nested schedule N={9,17,33,65}; stable Clenshaw–Curtis weights; GL256 reference; GL512 targeted replay of the worst rows.
- Atomic moments: independent double precision, Wolfram symbolic identities, and >=80-digit special-function/numerical fallback.
- Partition: nonnegativity, zero support, common-flux closure, macro/group moment sums, conditional uniqueness proof.
- Geometry: differential-opacity primary lane plus finite-cell `1-exp(-tau)` auditor; Jeans construction is geometry-only.
- Thermal: separate photon-number and energy ledgers; bounded thin/thick hardening coordinate.
- Exact zeros: below-threshold cross sections and primary G3/HeII rows exactly zero.
- No production history is integrated in this stage.
