# Literature evidence ledger

- Mellema et al., *C2-Ray: A new method for photon-conserving transport of ionizing radiation*, arXiv:astro-ph/0508416. Load-bearing use: absorbed ionizing-photon depletion must equal caused photoionizations, and analytic ionization relaxation can be coupled to RT.
- Friedrich et al., *Radiative transfer of energetic photons: X-rays and helium ionization in C2-Ray*, arXiv:1201.0602 / MNRAS 421, 2232. Load-bearing use: H/He multifrequency heating requires an optical-depth-aware energy moment and temperature convergence is generally stricter than ionization convergence.
- Verner et al., *Atomic data for astrophysics. II. New analytic fits for photoionization cross sections of atoms and ions*, ApJ 465, 487 (1996), DOI 10.1086/177435. Load-bearing use: analytic H I, He I and He II ground-state photoionization cross sections.
- Xiang, He & Wang, *On Fast Implementation of Clenshaw-Curtis and Fejer-type Quadrature Rules*, arXiv:1311.0445. Load-bearing use: stable Chebyshev-point interpolatory integration rather than ill-conditioned monomial Vandermonde inversion.
