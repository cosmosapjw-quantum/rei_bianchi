Both pinned harness archives were hash-locked and validated before production
calculation.

research harness SHA-256:
9adde688f8020e7feb2c1c0304b3204dbe70dd01e2d87e64a5c4eb357c019934

coding harness SHA-256:
6e67e999a0c19f6ed9de7c339067cc11691d5cf5cb662a11756d8fc393c849b4

The research loop separated hypotheses H1–H5, earliest certificates, claim
boundaries and external evidence. The coding harness enforced RED–GREEN tests,
transactional rollback, parity benchmarks and preservation of failed attempts.
See the receipts directory for validator outputs and task-level RED/GREEN logs.
