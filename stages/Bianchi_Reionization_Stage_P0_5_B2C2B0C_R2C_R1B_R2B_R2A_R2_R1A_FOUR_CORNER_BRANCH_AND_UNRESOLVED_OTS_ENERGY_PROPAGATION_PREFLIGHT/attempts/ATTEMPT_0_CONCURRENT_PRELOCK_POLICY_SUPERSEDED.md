   coefficients, not automatically to a nonlinear integrated trajectory.
   A narrow four-corner spread cannot authorize production without an
   independent monotonicity or validated interval certificate.

policies), the original 2e-4 numerical-scale uncertainty gate from the approved
design, and a separate continuous-parameter certification gate.
