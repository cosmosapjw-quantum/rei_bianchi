# Independent adversarial review

## Question

Could the apparent pass be an artifact of renormalizing away a genuine opacity or capacity inconsistency?

## Finding

The authoritative total opacity/current is inherited rather than fitted. Conditional normalization changes only the owner partition, and all owner shares are checked against the independently stored total. The raw-amplitude mismatch is disclosed as a non-load-bearing auditor. The rejected unsplit model is tested with the same total current and fails all 20 reachable first-substep comparisons, while the owner-correct model passes all 225 H/He certificates.

## Additional attacks

- **Hidden clipping:** none; failed comparison trajectories terminate.
- **Negative allocation:** zero rows.
- **Zero-support leakage:** zero rows.
- **Post-hoc prior choice:** none; all historical priors are auditors only.
- **Subgrid heat leakage:** source coefficient is exact integer zero.
- **Timestep masking:** integrated owner totals agree over `1,2,4,8` subdivisions to 2.688e-16.
- **Symbolic algebra:** Wolfram gives zero sum residuals and exact structural zeros.
- **Independent arithmetic:** Decimal-90 replay passes all 340 group cases.

## Claim boundary

The review supports an owner-correct preflight pass and authorization of the next fixed-point stage. It does not support a production chemistry/thermal-history claim.
