# HYPOTHESIS GRAPH

Generated only after the independent claim–source audit. `H-001` and `H-002` are the only serious candidates admitted to Phase 5/6. A candidate is a testable research architecture, not an implemented cure.

## H-001 — Active-face, verified, hybrid structure-preserving solver

CORE_CLAIM: A corrected solver organized around the physical H/He closed simplices can mitigate the numerical/scientific blocker core if it (a) fixes opacity-share normalization, (b) evolves interior states with a qualifying nonautonomous PDS scheme but switches exact invariant faces to separately proved reduced systems, (c) uses genuinely outward-rounded algebraic certification, (d) exploits the fixed-branch block-plus-rank-`<=3` nonlocal coupling only after factorization/conditioning checks, (e) controls branch-correct defect and physical QoIs, and (f) treats table knots as hybrid events with conservative restart.

MINIMAL_ASSUMPTIONS:

- Every smooth branch admits an explicit, nonnegative production–destruction decomposition with physically bound H/He content vectors and valid nonautonomous order conditions.
- An exact-zero face is used only when all absent-species production channels vanish there; otherwise the full closed-cone system activates that species without a floor.
- Primitive photon/energy/source terms and event functions can be independently assembled from the same declared physical model without reusing the candidate algebra.
- Qualified outward-rounded operations and verified elementary functions are available; whole-box derivatives enclose every derivative on a fixed branch.
- Event surfaces and simultaneous-event priority are model-authorized.

MECHANISM:

1. Re-derive `HI/HeI/HeII` shares directly from number-density opacities; compute each nonnegative 54 eV share directly and normalize instead of subtracting a complement.
2. Store conservative populations, not only logits; maintain H and He inventories as explicit content vectors. Use face-specific reduced systems for structural zeros and explicit vacuum/trace regimes.
3. On each smooth branch, factor the implicit Jacobian into local site/species/thermal blocks plus nonlocal feedback through the three global absorber sums; use a verified Schur/Woodbury solve with fail-closed conditioning checks.
4. Replace heuristic interval padding with directed/outward-rounded primitives, verified elementary functions, full residual/Jacobian inclusion, and strict self-inclusion. Algebraic certification remains distinct from trajectory error.
5. Reconstruct a branch-correct continuous path, evaluate an independently assembled defect, propagate stability/adjoint weights to owner-defined physical QoIs, and retain full/two-half distance only as a diagnostic.
6. Isolate all simple roots on the reconstructed path, run grazing/repeated-root falsifiers, order/coalesce simultaneous events by explicit semantics, roll back to the enclosed root, rebuild all branch/table-dependent physics, and restart while preserving inventories/reservoir custody.

KNOWN_LIMITS:

- No checked theorem directly covers this entire nonautonomous MPRK–SDIRK, interval, hybrid, radiative/thermal composition.
- Standard MPRK positivity does not prove exact-zero safety; active faces need new derivations and near-zero adversarial tests.
- Polynomial root completeness is only relative to its reconstruction; a true-solution enclosure and multiplicity strategy remain necessary.
- Rank `<=3` does not imply a well-conditioned or faster solve; interval dependency may erase the benefit.
- It does not cure runtime/provenance/package/resume/resource blockers; H-002 is independently necessary.

DISTINCTIVE_PREDICTIONS:

- Pure-He II attenuation approaches one `YHE` factor, not `YHE^2`; all pure-absorber limits give share 1 to the present absorber and 0 to absent absorbers.
- Exact invariant faces remain exact without floors when their production channels vanish; when production turns on, the face transition is explicit and inventory-preserving.
- The exact signed-sum and both Krawczyk witnesses fail closed or enclose the exact results.
- Primitive photon and energy ledgers can detect injected common-mode omissions that current tautologies miss.
- Refinement reduces independently evaluated defect/QoI error with the predicted order on smooth branches; event-time error converges separately.
- Block-plus-low-rank and dense reference solves agree within verified bounds; speedup occurs only in well-conditioned regimes.

COMPETING_HYPOTHESES: H-003 active-set DAE, H-004 asymptotic regime reduction, H-005 minimal patching, and generic solver replacement.

DECISIVE_TEST: The nine-field package in `work/VERIFICATION_PACKAGE.md` must pass pure/trace/vacuum limits, exact containment witnesses, nonautonomous PDS order/face invariance, primitive-ledger mutation detection, smooth and hybrid QoI convergence, all-root/grazing/simultaneous-event cases, dense-versus-low-rank equivalence/conditioning, and independent reference comparisons in an exact runtime.

FATAL_VULNERABILITY: Any admissible branch that lacks a valid PDS/face decomposition, any false enclosure under the qualified backend, any missed topology-changing event, or any physical QoI that fails its independently specified budget blocks the integrated solver architecture.

SUPPORTING_EVIDENCE: C-002–C-015 after audit; primary PDS, stoichiometry, M-matrix, Krawczyk, defect/QoI, photon-conservation, and event literature with their stated limits.

THREATENING_EVIDENCE: Current He II/interval/Krawczyk counterexamples; MPRK near-zero pathologies; absent trajectory reconstruction, energy oracle, active event restart, and conditioning data.

STATUS: `PROMOTED_AS_SPECIFIED_RESEARCH_ARCHITECTURE_ONLY`; unimplemented, unvalidated, and no solver/science promotion.

## H-002 — Dual-lane independent physical admission and custody

CORE_CLAIM: Scientific recovery requires two non-interchangeable lanes: a frozen forensic-compatibility lane that can reproduce historical BDF/current outputs without scientific authority, and a corrected-science lane admitted only by independent physical oracles, exact runtime/source identity, typed success/event/resume semantics, and bounded artifact custody.

MINIMAL_ASSUMPTIONS:

- Historical products must remain immutable for comparison, while corrected upstream generation is allowed only as a new identity.
- Independent limiting/manufactured/reference cases can be specified from primitive H/He/radiative physics without importing candidate outputs.
- Every external process, file, runtime, transition, and physical result can carry a typed identity and bounded admission record.
- Operational gates are allowed to fail closed and cannot be waived by physical plausibility or parity.

MECHANISM:

1. Label historical replay/parity as `FORENSIC_COMPATIBILITY_ONLY`; never use it as a corrected-science oracle.
2. Build a separately implemented primitive-flux/reference lane with analytic pure-absorber, equilibrium/linearized, manufactured branch, and systematic-refinement cases; record code/data independence.
3. Admit a solver result only after expected terminal/event state, finite/domain/simplex checks, residual and verified enclosure status, primitive photon/population/energy checks, QoI budgets, and complete diagnostics.
4. Bind exact source/tree/runtime/tool/candidate/validator/output identities; make terminal states absorbing and distinguish resume/restart/new generation.
5. Enforce allowlisted path/package membership, symlink-safe custody, streaming quotas, hard memory/disk/inode/wall/output limits, and incremental ancestry.

KNOWN_LIMITS:

- Architecture cannot repair H-001's formula or numerical defects; it only prevents their outputs from being over-admitted.
- Manufactured/analytic cases verify exercised equations, not empirical validation of the physical model.
- A reference lane can share conceptual errors unless primitive equations, implementation, and reviewers are genuinely independent.
- Several controls are software/provenance obligations rather than physics-specific mechanisms.

DISTINCTIVE_PREDICTIONS:

- Historical parity may remain green while corrected pure-He II/ledger/interval tests fail, and the system records compatibility without science promotion.
- Candidate mutation, runtime drift, terminal-resume ambiguity, out-of-generation package members, symlink escapes, and quota overrun all fail before scientific results are consumed.
- Primitive/reference tests detect at least the known extra-`YHE`, ledger self-cancellation, and event-custody defects independently of candidate output formatting.
- Corrected upstream BDF/reference artifacts receive new lineage and never overwrite or silently redefine historical calibration.

COMPETING_HYPOTHESES: One-lane in-place repair, same-code parity plus refinement, and governance-only receipts without physical predicates.

DECISIVE_TEST: The nine-field package must demonstrate a complete typed transition matrix and mutation campaign, exact environment/tool custody, independent oracle provenance, known-defect detection, absorbing terminal semantics, package/path attacks, resource exhaustion, and the explicit non-promotion of forensic parity.

FATAL_VULNERABILITY: If the corrected lane reuses the candidate's primitive algebra/code path, or any failure/identity/status can be serialized as admitted success, the claimed independence and scientific firewall collapse.

SUPPORTING_EVIDENCE: C-011–C-020 after audit; verification/validation literature distinguishing manufactured tests, code comparison, reproduction, and validated trajectories.

THREATENING_EVIDENCE: No authenticated independent reference lane exists; live runtime already mismatches; exploit effects for all eight `S` rows were not executed.

STATUS: `PROMOTED_AS_SPECIFIED_RESEARCH_ARCHITECTURE_ONLY`; unimplemented, no independent lane exists, and no solver/science promotion.

## H-003 — Complementarity / index-1 active-set DAE at physical faces

CORE_CLAIM: Exact zeros, vacuum/trace transitions, and table boundaries might be modeled as complementarity constraints or an index-1 DAE rather than multiple hand-written charts.

MINIMAL_ASSUMPTIONS: A consistent algebraic constraint manifold, unique active set, index-1 regularity, and physically authorized switching law exist across every branch.

MECHANISM: Enforce nonnegative species with complementary production/constraint multipliers; solve differential variables and active constraints together; switch active sets at certified events.

KNOWN_LIMITS: No audited primary source or current derivation establishes index, uniqueness, order, positivity, or compatibility with the current PDS/interval map. Complementarity can add nonsmoothness and event chatter.

DISTINCTIVE_PREDICTIONS: Exact faces are represented without arbitrary floors, and active-set transitions coincide with physically derived production-onset surfaces.

COMPETING_HYPOTHESES: H-001's explicit face-specific reduced systems.

DECISIVE_TEST: Derive the full constraint Jacobian/index and solve canonical zero→active, active→zero, and simultaneous-face cases with unique, inventory-preserving transitions and convergence.

FATAL_VULNERABILITY: Singular/ambiguous active sets or Zeno/chattering behavior.

SUPPORTING_EVIDENCE: C-007 establishes the boundary problem only.

THREATENING_EVIDENCE: No audited method-to-code support; current event system lacks production restart.

STATUS: hold for evidence; not a serious Phase-6 candidate.

## H-004 — Physics asymptotic/equilibrium regime reduction

CORE_CLAIM: Explicit vacuum, trace, optically thin/thick, and fast-equilibrium regimes could replace ill-conditioned full solves with analytic/asymptotic reduced maps and furnish independent oracles.

MINIMAL_ASSUMPTIONS: Small parameters and timescale separations are identifiable, uniform error bounds exist through transitions, and regime ownership is model-authorized.

MECHANISM: Select a reduced physical balance in certified parameter regions; match back to the full system conservatively; reuse limits as oracle cases.

KNOWN_LIMITS: No current quantitative scale-separation map or uniform error bound was found. Hard regime switching creates additional events and may miss intermediate physics.

DISTINCTIVE_PREDICTIONS: Reduced and full solutions agree within an asymptotic error envelope away from transition layers while stiffness/cost falls.

COMPETING_HYPOTHESES: H-001's single full closed-cone system and H-003 active-set DAE.

DECISIVE_TEST: Derive dimensionless parameters and uniform residual/QoI bounds, then validate overlap/matching across all regime interfaces.

FATAL_VULNERABILITY: No clean scale separation or transition-layer error dominates the QoI budget.

SUPPORTING_EVIDENCE: Pure-absorber and trace/vacuum limiting cases are valid diagnostic physics.

THREATENING_EVIDENCE: Exact vacuum/trace conventions and quantitative budgets are missing.

STATUS: hold for evidence; useful oracle seed, not a serious Phase-6 candidate.
